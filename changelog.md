## v0.3.0
- Joining a room from a different level now opens (or creates) a level with the room's level name and loads the host's objects into it. Your own level is saved and left untouched.
- Fixed a crash when someone edited or deleted an object you had selected. Remote changes now wait until you deselect it.
- Big levels now load faster when you join.
- Level names and other text with quotes or symbols no longer break syncing.
- Leaving a room now also stops it rejoining automatically next time.
